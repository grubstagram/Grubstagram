Stitch a lunch and stir it right,
Cyclops eyeball, wobbly, bright!
Troll toenails clatter, crunchy and yellow,
Goblin bogeys, green and mellow.
A witch's old sock, darned at the heel,
Lends the lot a whiff of last week's meal.
Cobweb floss in a sticky grey fluff,
Swamp sludge splat, and that's enough!
Dragon scales jingle, clink and chime,
Pantomime bolt, and lunch is on time!

Swipe for the spell 👉
