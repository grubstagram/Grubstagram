Stir the pot, stir the pot, in goes a boot,
Add a gutter-gunk dollop and a tatty tree root.
Tip in soggy leaves, brown and curled,
And the smelliest socks in the whole wide world.
Pencil shavings next, and a bobble hat's bobble,
Stir till it glops and begins to wobble.
Top it with twigs and a splat of pond slime,
Then serve it to trolls at pudding time!

Swipe for the spell 👉
