In goes a doormat, soggy and grey,
To wobble the sirens' sulks away.
Six odd socks, all whiffy and damp,
Plop, plop, plop, into the swamp!
Gutter gloop by the bucket, glug glug,
With a twist of old twine and a scrap of rug.
Three trainer tongues, all flappy and floppy,
Stir till the lot goes gloopy and sloppy.
A pinch of fluff and some lawn clippings too,
Now wibble and wobble. The jelly is due!

Swipe for the spell 👉
