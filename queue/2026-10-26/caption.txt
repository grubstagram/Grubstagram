Round the cauldron, stamp and hum,
In plops a pumpkin head, hollow and glum.
Three green ladles of goblin snot,
Stirred till it wobbles, a gloopy lot.
Thirteen toenails from a troll's big feet,
Crunch them up for a crusty treat.
Swamp sludge next, and my old grey sock,
Wrung out slowly round the clock.
A sprinkle of scales, a web of fluff,
Moon-drizzle over: that's quite enough!

Swipe for the spell 👉
