Into the pot goes a boot, left and sloppy,
With forty damp leaves all gooey and floppy!
Add a bucket of puddle, all brown and all slurpy,
Then a bogey-green glove that goes burpy-burpy!
Twist in some twigs that are furry with mould,
And a sock-coloured string that's a hundred years old!
Sprinkle some crumbs from the compost heap's floor,
Stir it up, stir it up, grumble and snore!
Troll family, troll family, gather to dine,
Elbows on table and wellies in line!

Swipe for the spell 👉
