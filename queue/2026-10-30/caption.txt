Thirteen witches round the pot, stirring up a stinky lot!
In go bogeys, green and thick, picked by goblins, nice and quick.
Troll toenails, nine, go crunch and clack, then a sock that's gone all black.
Swamp sludge slops in, glug by glug, with a cyclops eyeball in each mug.
Dragon scales go ping and pop, cobweb floss goes on the top.
Moonbeam pus, a splash, a squirt, wipe your nose upon your shirt!
Stir it widdershins, thirteen times, and cackle out these gruesome rhymes.
Gloop, gloop, gloop, the brew is done. Who wants the sock? There's one for everyone!
