Round the rim and widdershins we creep,
Troll toenails in, a crusty heap.
Cyclops eyeballs, plop, plop, plop,
Bobbing round the slimy top.
Goblin bogeys, stringy and green,
The grottiest jar you've ever seen.
Scoop of sludge from an ogre's boot,
Then my old sock, a smelly hoot.
Cobweb fluff and a dragon-scale sprinkle,
Stir till the whole lot starts to twinkle.
